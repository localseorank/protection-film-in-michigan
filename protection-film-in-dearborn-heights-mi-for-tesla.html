Tesla PPF Cleaning and Long-Term Care Guidelines

Following proper maintenance protocols ensures that paint protection film on a Tesla remains clear, glossy, and fully functional throughout its service life. Adhering to simple care habits prevents premature edge lifting and staining.

Wash the vehicle regularly using touchless car wash facilities or gentle hand washing with dedicated pH-neutral automotive soaps and plush microfiber towels.

  1. 1Allow the film to cure undisturbed for seven days before the first exterior car wash.
  2. 2Avoid directing high-pressure sprayers closer than eighteen inches to panel edges.
  3. 3Clean bird droppings, bug splatter, and tree sap promptly to prevent clear coat etching.
  4. 4Top the film with a dedicated ceramic coating to enhance hydrophobicity and dirt repellency.

Should fine wash scratches appear on the film, parking the Tesla outside on a warm sunny afternoon activates the self-healing topcoat, smoothing the surface back to a mirror-like finish.
